Keeping Your Hanahan Water Heater Running Longer

Many premature tank failures trace back to maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Flush the tank every year to clear sediment, get the anode rod checked every few years, and operate the relief valve annually so it doesn't seize. Keep the temperature near 120 degrees to reduce scald risk and slow scale buildup. For a tankless heater in a hard-water area, plan on descaling once a year so the heat exchanger stays clean.

Take a look at the age of your water heater. The rating plate on the tank usually encodes the month and year it was built, and its age tells you a lot. Most tank models make it to 8 to 12 years, heat pump units 10 to 15, and regularly descaled tankless units about 20. If your heater is past that range and is also showing discolored water, popping sounds, or dampness under the tank, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than a rushed emergency install once the tank fails.

Left alone, a small problem tends to grow. Consider a spent anode rod: swapping it out is a short, inexpensive repair, but if nobody replaces it, the water begins corroding the tank itself, and suddenly a minor fix has become a full replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and spares you a flooded utility room.

Common Water Heater Problems in Hanahan

Whatever type of water heater your Hanahan home has, it tends to fail in a predictable way. Gas tanks tend to have trouble with pilot assemblies and thermocouples, dirty burners, and venting that doesn't draft. Electric tanks burn out elements and thermostats, particularly when sediment buries the lower element. Tankless units show error codes for scale buildup, ignition faults, and flow sensor trouble, and many of those trace back to skipped descaling. Heat pump water heaters need clean air filters and a clear condensate drain, and they struggle in cramped closets. We service all four types regularly and carry the parts to fix most problems on the first visit.
