Safety

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to provide safe conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected water heater can result in carbon monoxide from a blocked or undersized vent, scalding at the tap, Legionella growth in water stored too cool, and costly water damage from a failed tank. Summerville Water Heater provides commercial installations and inspections that cover venting and combustion air, gas piping sizing, relief valve and discharge line routing, expansion tanks, thermostatic mixing valves, drain pans, seismic strapping where required, and permits and inspections. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and health or building inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test results, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms.
